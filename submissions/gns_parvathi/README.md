## My artifact

**Track B** — before/after writeup:

### The task
Writing a weekly status report for my manager, in the same format every Friday.

### Before
I used Claude as a plain chat tool. Every week I re-explained the report's sections, structure, and tone, and the draft came back generic. I then edited it to sound like my usual reports, and pasted in client background each time.

### After (with Claude)
I now state the goal, audience, and constraints up front, and keep the report format and client background documents in one place instead of re-explaining them. Claude drafts the report; I decide the recommendations and anything client-specific, and I check facts, dates, and tone before sending.

### What I learned
- Generic output usually means missing context, so the fix is to give Claude the audience and constraints, not to re-ask.
- Claude sounds fluent even when it is wrong, so I stay accountable for verifying what goes out.
