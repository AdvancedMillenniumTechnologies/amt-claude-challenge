## After: AI Fluency — Framework & Foundations

This course gave me a mental model for working with AI well: the **4D Framework**.

| The "D" | What it means | How I apply it |
|---|---|---|
| **Delegation** | Deciding *what* to do with AI, *whether* to use it, and how to split the work between me and the AI | I ask: is this task a good fit for AI? What's my goal, and which parts need my judgment? |
| **Description** | Communicating clearly with AI: describing the product I want, the process I'd like it to follow, and how it should behave | I specify the output, the approach, and the tone/role up front instead of hoping for the best |
| **Discernment** | Critically evaluating what AI gives back: the output, its reasoning, and how the interaction is going | I check for accuracy, gaps, and made-up details before trusting or using anything |
| **Diligence** | Taking responsibility for how I create with, share, and deploy AI-assisted work | I verify important claims, am transparent about AI's role, and own the final result |

**Key ideas I'm keeping**

- AI fluency is a **loop**, not a one-time prompt: delegate, describe, discern, then refine.
- Good results come from **clear communication plus critical review**, not from either alone.
- I stay **accountable** for anything I put my name on, even if AI helped produce it.
